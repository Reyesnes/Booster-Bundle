# Content & Ads

Working space for Booster Bundle's content creation, ad prep, and social output.

- `brand-voice.md` — tone/voice reference. Fill this in before producing final customer-facing copy.
- `calendar/` — content calendars (per platform or unified).
- `ads/` — ad copy and creative briefs (Facebook, Google, LinkedIn, UGC).
- `social/` — carousels, posts, and other social-native content.

See `.claude/rules/content-strategy.md` and the `content-strategist` subagent.
